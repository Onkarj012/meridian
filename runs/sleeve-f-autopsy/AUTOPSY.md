## What this does and does not show

This is an autopsy of three rule-family grid campaigns. It does **not** evaluate the original LightGBM router: `models/lightgbm.py` is a stub, and no router result is represented here.

The runner supplied no promotion flags, so `beats_baselines` is reported as `not_evaluated`; a stored `false` means no evidence, not a failed baseline test. Also, `hit_rate` is the positive-net-trade rate after cost, while `touch_rate` is the target-touch share. They are not interchangeable and should not be conflated.
