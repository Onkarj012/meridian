## CONFIDENCE FILTERING

### Descriptive reporting

**Permitted.**

The principal should receive a comprehensive retrospective results table covering:

- raw predictions and confusion matrices
- raw and balanced accuracy
- actionable non-FLAT accuracy
- confidence coverage at top 50%, 25%, and 10%
- confidence-filtered accuracy with row counts
- calibration, ECE, decile accuracy, and fold dispersion
- raw and smoothed-target results when a smooth study eventually exists
- price/magnitude results against honest baselines

Every confidence-filtered number must be labeled:

> Retrospective descriptive analysis. Thresholds were examined after outcomes were known. Not an operationally validated policy.

The monotone pooled pattern is real as a description. It is not equivalent to stable calibration: V1-C failed the registered `0.80` worst-fold Spearman requirement at both horizons, and v0 H60 confidence was nearly constant on 2025H1.

### Operational filtering

**Not presently permitted. A new prospective registration is mandatory.**

“Top-X%” cannot be computed using the future test distribution. It must become a fixed numerical threshold learned before evaluation.

Minimal honest registration:

1. **Model:** exact frozen v0 architecture and features; no feature, seed, loss, or hyperparameter changes.
2. **Training:** through `2025-03-24`.
3. **Calibration and threshold fitting:** `2025-04-01` through `2025-06-23`.
4. **Primary policy:** per-horizon confidence cutoff equal to the calibration-set 75th percentile, producing an intended top-25% policy.
5. **Secondary policy:** calibration-set 90th percentile, report-only. It cannot replace the primary policy after evaluation.
6. **Actions:** only predicted `UP` or `DOWN`; non-overlapping decisions; fixed next-bar entry, horizon exit, costs, sizing, and overlap handling.
7. **No policy selection** from the burned dev folds, 2025H1 outcomes, or final results.

Required gates for the primary policy, per horizon:

- ECE: H15 `≤ 0.030`; H60 `≤ 0.050`
- confidence-decile Spearman: `≥ 0.80`
- top-minus-bottom decile accuracy: `≥ 0.08`
- filtered exact-class accuracy improvement over unfiltered actionable accuracy: `≥ 0.030`
- paired 2,000-draw session-bootstrap 95% lower bound for that improvement: `> 0`
- realized coverage: `15%–35%`
- non-overlapping accepted observations: H15 `≥ 500`; H60 `≥ 150`
- net mean return after frozen costs: `> 0`
- session-bootstrap 95% lower bound for net mean return: `> 0`
- net annualized Sharpe: `≥ 0.50`

Passing would license forward shadow operation, not live capital. A separate registered shadow-to-live gate would still be required.

## SMOOTHED STUDY

### Existing smoothing

The proposed intuition is partly represented already. V0/V1 contain:

- 5-, 15-, 30-, and 60-minute returns
- multi-window realized volatility and ATR
- VWAP deviation and slope
- an EMA9/EMA21 slope
- 30-minute basis change
- multi-window spot and BANKNIFTY returns
- regime features derived from rolling trend and volatility state

These are causal low-pass summaries. Therefore, “add an EMA” alone is not a structurally new information class and should not be presented as such.

### Study verdict

**A smoothed direction target is structurally distinct and worth one tightly bounded prospective study. A larger smoothed-feature search is not.**

The genuine new hypothesis is that the desired object is the direction of an underlying forward path rather than the noisy endpoint return. That changes the estimand. It does not reopen magnitude or price prediction.

### Frozen v1.1-smooth skeleton

For each horizon \(h\), define a forward-only smoothed label:

\[
Q_0=F_t,\qquad
Q_j=\alpha_hF_{t+j}+(1-\alpha_h)Q_{j-1}
\]

with:

- H15 half-life: `5 minutes`
- H60 half-life: `15 minutes`
- \(\alpha_h=1-2^{-1/\text{half-life}}\)

Define the smoothed return as:

\[
r^{smooth}_{h,t}=\log(Q_h/F_t)
\]

Apply the existing volatility-scaled UP/FLAT/DOWN band unchanged. Require every class to represent at least `15%` of evaluation rows; otherwise the target is invalidated as excessively FLAT.

Frozen ladder:

- **S0:** frozen v0 features, raw target; comparator
- **S1:** v0 plus the fixed causal EMA stack, raw target; input-only diagnostic
- **S2:** v0 features, smoothed target; target-only diagnostic
- **S3:** fixed EMA stack plus smoothed target; precommitted production candidate

The EMA stack is limited to:

- price-to-EMA gaps at half-lives `5` and `15`
- five-minute slopes of those two EMAs
- EMA5-to-EMA15 spread

All inputs must use same-session bars available at or before the decision timestamp. EMAs reset at the session boundary and require a `45-minute` warm-up. No candidate may replace S3 after outcomes are inspected.

### Leakage prohibitions

- No centered or two-sided smoothing.
- No input window may include any bar after the decision timestamp.
- External inputs remain restricted through `t-1`.
- The label may use only `t+1` through `t+h`; it may not extend beyond the prediction horizon.
- No symmetric future window around `t+h`.
- No smoothing across sessions.
- No post-results change to half-lives, flat bands, feature set, or production candidate.

### Honest evaluation data

The five development folds and 2025H1 are burned for selection. They may support implementation validation and clearly labeled descriptive diagnostics only.

The honest choices are:

- prospectively accruing data beginning after the new registration freeze; preferred, or
- the one-shot sealed 2025H2 test; immediate but expensive and systemically valuable.

The preferred first evaluation is at least `60` new trading sessions. It must be frozen before the first eligible observation and evaluated non-overlapping as well as minute-pooled.

Prospective pass gates for S3:

- smoothed-label balanced accuracy `≥ 0.400`
- smoothed-label macro AUC `≥ 0.560`
- raw endpoint-label balanced accuracy `≥ 0.370`
- raw endpoint-label macro AUC `≥ 0.540`
- raw endpoint BA improvement over S0 `≥ 0.003`
- each raw-direction gate passes in both halves of the evaluation period
- confidence gates remain ECE `≤ 0.030/0.050`, Spearman `≥ 0.80`, and top-minus-bottom `≥ 0.08`
- primary top-25% filtered accuracy lift `≥ 0.030`, with paired session-bootstrap 95% lower bound `> 0`

A pass licenses one confirmatory sealed evaluation or shadow continuation. It does not license trading. Failure of any mandatory leg kills v1.1-smooth without an EMA or target sweep.

The known failure modes transfer: smoothing can make labels easier while producing no endpoint-return or economic edge; increase FLAT prevalence; create overlapping-label dependence; and manufacture apparent accuracy by attenuating moves toward zero. That is why raw endpoint performance remains a mandatory co-gate.

## PRICE PREDICTIONS

**Do not restore `predicted_close`, signed return, or price-level prediction. The output contract remains direction plus confidence only.**

Absolute-move output also remains suppressed for the present information class. V1’s `median_abs_move_bps` failed its registered MAE gates, despite positive H15 rank IC.

The evidence is decisive:

- H15 predicted-close advantage over “no change” was about `0.07%`, economically negligible.
- H60 was worse than “no change.”
- Signed Huber magnitude failed systematically.
- Reformed nonnegative magnitude then failed mandatory MAE and non-overlapping gates.

Smoothing the same price history is not structurally new information and does not reopen this question.

Price or absolute-move prediction may reopen only after introducing a genuinely new information class, such as options-surface state, order-book data, or verified event/news timing, under a new registration requiring:

- MAE skill `≥ 2%` over the best fixed baseline
- positive skill in at least `4/5` development folds
- non-overlapping MAE skill `≥ 1%`
- daily IC `≥ 0.05`
- positive-IC day fraction `≥ 0.55`
- sealed-test paired-bootstrap 95% lower bound for MAE skill `> 0`

Anything weaker remains research-only and must not be exposed as a price forecast.
